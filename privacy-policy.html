Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2024

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how WordFrag collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

WordFrag is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information necessary to provide you with the best puzzle experience.

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: If you choose to create an account, we may collect your email address and username.
  • Contact Information: When you contact us for support, we collect the information you provide in your message.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Game Data: Your puzzle progress, daily streak, statistics, and gameplay history.
  • Device Information: Device type, iOS version, and app version for troubleshooting and optimization.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features you use, and time spent playing.

Information We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other personal files.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Game Functionality

To provide you with daily puzzles, track your progress, maintain your streak, and display statistics.

Personalization

To customize your experience, remember your preferences, and improve puzzle difficulty matching.

Communication

To respond to your inquiries, send important updates about the app, and notify you of new features.

Analytics and Improvement

To understand how users interact with WordFrag, identify bugs, and improve app performance and features.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect your information.

Local Storage

Most of your game data (puzzles, progress, statistics) is stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage mechanisms. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

Cloud Storage (Optional)

If you enable iCloud sync, your game data is stored in your personal iCloud account, which is controlled by Apple and subject to their privacy policy.

Security Measures

  • Data encryption in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited employee access to user data

4. Third-Party Services

WordFrag may use third-party services to provide and improve our app. These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your information.

Services We May Use

Apple App Store

For app distribution, in-app purchases, and device authentication.

Analytics Services

To understand app usage patterns and improve user experience (anonymized data only).

Crash Reporting

To identify and fix bugs and crashes that affect app performance.

Note: We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share data with service providers necessary to operate WordFrag, and they are contractually obligated to protect your information.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Correction

Request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete data.

Deletion

Request deletion of your personal data and account.

Data Portability

Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format.

Opt-Out

Opt out of marketing communications and data collection.

Object

Object to certain types of data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at wordfraggame@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

WordFrag is designed for users aged 12 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 12.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 12 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at wordfraggame@gmail.com, and we will take steps to remove that information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and notify you through the app or via email. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: wordfraggame@gmail.com
Contact Form: Submit a privacy request

We are committed to protecting your privacy and will respond to all legitimate requests within 30 days.
